## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the AI Jewelry Try-On Booth ("the Booth"). The Booth provides a virtual augmented reality (AR) preview of selected jewelry pieces (including earrings and necklaces) using real-time video processing. By touching the screen to start and accepting the privacy notice, you agree to these Terms and Conditions.

## 2. Privacy Policy & Video Processing

1. **Local Processing Only**: The camera stream and facial tracking points used to anchor virtual jewelry are processed entirely in real-time within the booth kiosk's local memory. No live camera feed, video recordings, facial scans, or biometric identifiers are transmitted to any cloud server or stored on the kiosk.
2. **No Facial Recognition**: The technology strictly detects facial contours and pose coordinates to position 3D jewelry models. We do not identify individuals, create facial templates, or link visitors to profiles.
3. **Photo Capture and Consent**:
   - Taking a photo is entirely voluntary.
   - When a photo is taken, it is displayed on the screen for your review.
   - If you tap "Discard" or "Retake", the image is immediately and permanently discarded from memory.
   - Only when you explicitly tap "Save" is the composited photo uploaded to our temporary server to generate your personal QR code.
4. **Retention & Deletion**:
   - Saved photos are stored temporarily with an expiration period of twenty-four (24) hours.
   - Photos and associated access tokens are automatically and permanently purged upon expiration.
   - You may delete your photo immediately at any time by scanning your QR code, opening your personal share page, and tapping "Delete my photo".
