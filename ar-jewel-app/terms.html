Privacy & Video Processing

  • Local Processing Only: Your live camera stream and facial tracking points stay in local memory on the booth screen. No video or biometric coordinates are recorded or uploaded.
  • No Biometric Identification: We do not identify individuals, create facial templates, or build user profiles.
  • Consent-Driven Photo Capture: Taking a photo is optional. If you decline or take no action, it is discarded immediately. Only when you explicitly tap Save is the photo uploaded to generate your private QR code.
  • Automated Deletion: Saved photos expire and are permanently deleted after the retention period (24 hours). You can also delete your photo immediately using the "Delete my photo" button on your share page.
  • Audience: Designed for adults, or children accompanied by a parent or guardian.
