Anvaya means the thread that connects — the link between one thing and the next. It is the right word for what we do: we carry four centuries of Indian goldsmithing into a medium that did not exist when those techniques were invented.

A kundan setting is judged by how uncut stones catch a moving light. A jhumka is judged by how its fringe swings. Neither survives a flat product photograph, which is why jewellery has stayed stubbornly offline while everything else moved.

We scan each piece at micron detail, rebuild its gold, its enamel and its stones as physically accurate materials, and place it back on a real person — on their own screen, in their own light. The karigar's work is unchanged. Only the distance between the bench and the buyer has closed.

We build this for houses that already have the craft: heritage jewellers, bridal ateliers, and the retail floors that carry them.

Our Technology

The Art of Jewellery, Reimagined.

3D Visualisation

Each piece photogrammetry-scanned and rebuilt with measured gold, enamel and stone response, so a polki reads as polki from every angle.

AR Try-On

Neck, ear, hand and wrist tracking tuned for jewellery — drape, weight and shadow behave the way the real piece would.

VR Showroom

A walkable atelier where collections sit under jewellers' lighting, shared live with a client who is three time zones away.

Immersive Storytelling

The provenance of a design — region, technique, the hands that made it — told alongside the piece instead of on a tag.
